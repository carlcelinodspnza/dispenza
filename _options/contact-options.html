Contact — four booking layouts

Four ways to lay out the booking area, each aimed at a different part of what is currently redundant. Nothing here is wired to the live page.

What is real copy and what is not. Verbatim from contact.html: every field label ("First Name *", "What is your website URL "www." *", …), all three store-type options, all three select options, the SUBMIT string, both rep names, the email atom hello@dispenza.com and the Las Vegas address. Written by me for these mockups and NOT owner-approved copy: the lede lines under each heading ("Tell us about the store…", "Six fields. We'll come back with a time.", "Pick who you'd like to talk to…"), the rep role label "Dispensary marketing", and option D's "No preference / We'll assign the next available" card. Treat those as layout filler — they need your wording before anything ships. One flag: the address appears on the live page in two conflicting forms — "Suite 110" and "Suite 110 B" — which build note F8 raised and is still an open owner question. I used "Suite 110" here; it is not a ruling on which is correct.
Measured on the live page today. Page is 3,119px tall. The first form field sits at y=1,416px — 1.6 viewports down, on a page whose only job is that form. Four headings run before any mechanism appears: CONTACT US → LET'S TALK → "Let's Talk About Your Dispensary Marketing Needs" → BOOK YOUR DEMO. There are four same-page jump links, and two of them scroll backwards — "Request a Demo Consultation" travels −1,645px and "Contact Dispenza for Marketing Solutions" −2,011px, i.e. the "Related Pages" block links to the page you are already on. "Select Rep" appears three times, and choosing a rep navigates away to sergio.html / thomas.html, abandoning the form — two conversion paths competing inside one section.

Option A

Split — pitch left, form right

The form moves up beside the copy instead of below it. The two "doors" collapse into plain contact facts, and the reps become a small "or ask for one of them" line rather than a section with its own heading and its own buttons.

Kills: the form is on screen at the section's first pixel, so every one of the four jump links has nothing left to do. Removes one heading level and the three "Select Rep" repetitions.
