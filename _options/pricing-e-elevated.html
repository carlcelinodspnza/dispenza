Option E — three elevation treatments

Same layout you picked: two equal cards, the paid-for modules promoted to tags, the shared service list beneath. Only the treatment changes — depth, surface and typographic weight. The flatness came from a single hairline border on plain white with no shadow, no tint and no hierarchy between the two cards.

Treatment 1

Lifted

Three stacked shadows instead of a border, a faint vertical wash so the card is not flat white, an accent bar across the hero's top edge, and module tags with their own gradient and drop. Hover raises the card. Most restrained — reads as the same design, better made.
